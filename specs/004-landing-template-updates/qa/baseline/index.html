What is the Workaround TCO Audit, and why $350?▼

My introductory 24–48h TDABC audit uncovers hidden manual labor payroll leaks (e.g. WhatsApp copy-paste, billing reconciliation) and bloated SaaS subscriptions.

Do you always build custom full-stack code from scratch?▼

No. I practice pragmatic architecture triage on Day 1. If an existing FOSS tool solves your backend needs without seat taxes, I won't waste your runway reinventing the wheel.

I only build custom applications when your business model genuinely requires proprietary workflows.

Why is UnderUndre called a "bureau" if projects are run by a single lead engineer?▼

A bureau is an institutionalized operating method and standard, not an inflated headcount. The bureau operates on the model of classical European engineering practices (Ingenieurbüro and Bureau d'études): standardized DDL contracts, rigid timeboxes (Fixed Scope Knife), and sovereign infrastructure delivered turn-key without intermediaries.

You pay for the architectural precision of the lead engineer (NACE 62.01) and direct accountability with the craftsman building the system — not for agency overhead, account managers, and corporate telephone games.

How does Upwork Direct Contracts Escrow protect my capital?▼

You deposit the flat fee into Upwork Direct Contracts escrow. The freelancer-side fee is 5%. For clients, US ACH payments carry no Upwork service fee; card payments incur the standard acquirer fee (3–5%). I never touch your capital upfront.

Funds are released only upon successful deployment and live verification during Day 14 onboarding.

Who owns the code, intellectual property, and hosting?▼

Split-IP model: the platform core (UnderUndre Core Engine) remains the exclusive property of the bureau — you receive a perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive license to operate it. 100% exclusive rights to the custom modules, DDL schemas, branding, and content created during your sprint transfer to you immediately upon escrow release, under applicable intellectual-property law (in the EU — Directive 2009/24/EC).

The custom repository is transferred directly to your GitHub, and servers remain entirely under your control. Activity classification: EU NACE Rev. 2, code 62.01.

What is covered under the 7-Day Bugfix SLA?▼

For 7 calendar days post-deployment I resolve incidents per SLA at zero charge: P1 (system outage) — within 24 hours, P2 (broken key workflow) — within 72 hours.
